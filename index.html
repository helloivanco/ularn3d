THE ADVENTURER’S FIELD GUIDE

        
Every step matters.

        

          Find the potion of cure dianthroritis on Volcano 5 and return home
          before 400 mobuls pass. The Eye of Larn, on Dungeon 15, reveals the
          demons guarding your way.
        

        

          

            
Explore & fight

            

              Arrow keys or the direction pad move you. Step into a creature to
              attack. Click a known tile to travel; travel stops at danger. Time
              advances with actions, not while you think.
            

          

          

            
Enter & descend

            

              Stand on a landmark and use its contextual button.
              E enters buildings, > descends, and
              < ascends. Use o to open doors and
              chests.
            

          

          

            
Prepare

            

              i inventory · w wield · W wear ·
              q drink · r read · d drop ·
              c cast. The town’s stores, bank, and college can help.
            

          

          

            
Survive

            

              Wait with . to recover slowly. Your expedition saves
              automatically between actions. Death ends the expedition.
              Reloading restores your last saved turn, not a new life.
            

          

        

        

          The complete Ularn rules are preserved. Press ? during play
          for the original command reference. Camera rotation never changes the
          compass directions.
