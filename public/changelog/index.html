Recent versions

From the current line.

Maintained alongside CHANGELOG.md in the repository. Older tags remain on GitHub Releases.

  1. v1.3.44

    Start screen fits the window

    • The play screen shows the class choices, the class note, Multiplayer, Continue, and Download for Windows together on a normal desktop window
    • A saved expedition no longer pushes the Windows download below the fold
  2. v1.3.43

    Clearer download links

    • Links under Download for Windows read About, What’s New, History, Verify Download, then a GitHub icon, each on one line
  3. v1.3.42

    Chat emoji

    • The multiplayer chat box has an emoji button that opens a compact picker
    • Choosing an emoji puts it in the message at the cursor. Enter still sends
  4. v1.3.41

    Public end-screen scoreboard

    • After an expedition ends, the scoreboard reads the same verified public board as the rest of the site
    • If that board has no rows, it says the board is empty. If the board cannot be loaded, it says so
  5. v1.3.40

    Clearer chrome

    • Multiplayer, the lobby, and the leaderboard close with a round X. The title stays
    • Links under Download for Windows read About, New, History, Verify, then a GitHub icon, each on one line
    • The spectating line sits under the header on a blurred dark chip. The number is people watching, not the players or the host
  6. v1.3.39

    Live multiplayer rooms

    • Hosting, joining, and watching open the room and keep the lobby, chat, and turns in sync
    • Ready and Start talk to the room. If the host is gone, the next player can take the room
    • A finished co-op run is sent in for a verified or rejected score, and the leaderboard shows verified rows
    • Chat history stays with the people still in the room
  7. v1.3.37

    Verified scores

    • A finished run is replayed on the server. The number on the public board is the score that replay produced
    • A run that is too long to replay is refused. Shorter runs still count
    • A changed log, a run used twice, someone else's run, a co-op score from anyone but the host, and a run that finished too fast are still refused
  8. v1.3.36

    Score submit checks

    • A co-op score submission is checked against the current host of that room
    • Knowing another player's run id does not throw that run away
    • Too many score submissions in an hour are refused, and the run stays as it was
  9. v1.3.35

    Unknown finds and the aura switch

    • Picking up a potion or a scroll no longer tells you what it is. Drinking, reading, and an identify scroll still do. The shop still names what you buy
    • The floor bubble is only there in a multiplayer game, and it starts on. AURA: ON / AURA: OFF hides it. Off means creatures do not take that bubble's turn
    • A solo expedition has no bubble and no switch. Creatures still take their usual turn
  10. v1.3.34

    Online co-op

    • Optional multiplayer: host or join a room, watch a game, and chat with the party
    • Solo play is the same expedition. Online menus say “Online unavailable” when the service cannot be reached
    • A verified leaderboard is ready for scores the server has replayed. Unverified runs stay off the board
  11. v1.3.33

    Cooperation aura

    • A flat bubble follows you on the dungeon floor. It is 20 tiles wide and 10 tiles tall, centered on you with the extra tile to the east and north
    • The bubble is the ellipse of that rectangle. A creature on your floor is inside when its tile sits in the ellipse. The corners of the rectangle are outside it
    • When you take a turn, only creatures inside your bubble take a turn. Creatures outside stay put, and a creature on another floor never acts
    • Each creature acts once for the person who just moved
    • The bubble is a flat unlit shape. Balanced play stays evenly lit: no fog, no bloom, no shadow map, and no extra light
    • Town is unchanged
  12. v1.3.32

    Clearer caves

    • Hall floors and the rock around them use different tints on the same stone. The floor reads lighter than the wall
    • Deeper caves and the volcano use a warmer tint on that stone
    • Stairs, doors, and the town exit carry a small unlit mark. Gold and items sit a little brighter on the pictures they already use
    • A flat dark disc sits under you and under creatures, and you have a thin outline. It is not a cast shadow
    • The wall that drops in front of the camera keeps a shorter lip
    • Balanced play stays evenly lit: no fog when you zoom out, no bloom, and no shadow maps
  13. v1.3.31

    Monster hover card

    • Hovering a creature you can already see opens a small card with its sprite, its name, and a short original note
    • Hidden creatures and mimics stay as they are: no card and no extra facts
    • Floor items keep the one-line ground label
    • Hit points stay off the card. The game does not reveal that number
  14. v1.3.30

    Reachable caves

    • Every cave and volcano floor can be walked from where you enter. A room or hallway with no way in is joined by a door, or it is not built
    • Original chart floors keep their rooms. A sealed room gets a door into the cavern you can already travel
    • Stairs, monsters, and items go down after that, and only on tiles you can reach
    • Rare-item chances, combat, town, and teleport landings are unchanged
  15. v1.3.29

    Rare find rates

    • Brass lamp is rarer (5%). Slayer is 5 points rarer at every depth it can appear, and still starts on dungeon 10
    • Staff of power is 2 points rarer at every depth, and still starts on dungeon 8
    • Sword of slashing is 7.5%. Elven chain and the orb of enlightenment are 8.333%. Those are the closest steps on the 1–120 roll
    • Other rare items, caves, doors, combat, spells, and shops are unchanged
  16. v1.3.28

    Teleport landings

    • Teleport stays on the map. A scroll, spell, trap, or elevator never drops you outside the level
    • In town, you land in town. The square is not a cave, and the rock around it is not a floor
    • You arrive on open floor with a step back into the level. Rock, a closed door, and a walled-off pocket are skipped
    • A bad roll picks another open tile, or you stay put. It does not leave you in the wall
    • Caves, doors, combat, and rare-item chances are unchanged
  17. v1.3.27

    Reachable floors

    • Generated caves are kept only when you can walk the whole floor. A roll that traps rooms behind solid rock is discarded and rolled again
    • Stairs, monsters, and items go down after that, on tiles you can reach
    • Original chart floors are not redrawn. Their rock stays, and the stairs sit on the cavern you can travel
    • No extra tunnels or corridor doors. A treasure room still has one door, and only when it opens onto the maze
    • Combat, textures, and save timing are unchanged
  18. v1.3.26

    Original caves, doors, and rare finds

    • Floors are irregular Ularn labyrinths again — not a smoothed or more connected cave
    • The same weird door layouts every run were fixed canned maps. Those maps keep a door only where the original chart has one
    • Ordinary generated floors do not grow corridor doors. A treasure room has a single door in its wall
    • Rare artifacts use their original odds again. The brass lamp is unchanged
    • Combat, textures, and save timing are unchanged
  19. v1.3.25

    Honest expedition-ended actions

    • After death, only working end-screen actions remain — New expedition, and Enter while the scoreboard is still opening
    • No more dead Continue / Return to game / Esc buttons on a finished run
    • Ambient wall rats, labyrinth doors, textured floors, quieter saves, journal cap, and classic loot unchanged
  20. v1.3.24

    Quiet rats on the walls

    • Small ambient rats scurry along dungeon wall tops — you cannot fight, loot, or click them
    • Only a handful appear at a time; town stays still
    • Labyrinth doors, textured floors, quieter saves, journal cap, XP panel, and classic loot unchanged
  21. v1.3.23

    Stable long expeditions

    • Long runs no longer slow the browser from an ever-growing journal — history stays deep and scrollable with a soft cap
    • Known-floor walks skip redundant map hashing; log snapshots reuse between paints
    • Textured caves, quiet saves, labyrinth doors, XP panel, and north camera unchanged
  22. v1.3.22

    Textured floors, quieter saves

    • Cave floors show classic stone texture again under flat lighting (no more solid white ground)
    • Walking no longer writes the expedition to disk every step — Save, exit, or leaving the tab persists
    • Labyrinth doors, XP panel, north camera, and classic loot density unchanged
  23. v1.3.21

    Door throats and labyrinth floors

    • Corridor doors seal flush with connecting stone — no diagonal keypad squeeze-arounds
    • Closed doors block corner-cuts; open a door to pass
    • Dungeon floors stay classic Ularn caverns but read more like a labyrinth (less blank stone, less wide-open halls)
    • Stairs, town exit, flat caves, XP panel, and classic loot density unchanged
  24. v1.3.20

    Smoother browser dungeon walking

    • Balanced graphics skips the shadow pass and tone-mapping cost that still hitch Chrome dungeon walks
    • Flat caves keep even lighting; stone floor maps restored in 1.3.22 after a temporary unmapped pass
    • Known floors reuse geometry and skip HUD/minimap thrash when only the adventurer moves
    • Dungeon walking is held to a town-relative frame budget in automated checks
  25. v1.3.19

    Experience panel, flat caves, north camera, door facing

    • Experience panel beside Spells and Stats with classic Ularn titles and XP progress
    • Even dungeon lighting — no torch hotspots, and zooming out no longer dims the cave
    • Camera starts facing north; your zoom and elevation preferences are remembered
    • Corridor doors face the hall correctly again
  26. v1.3.18

    Classic caverns, reachable stairs, remembered name

    • Classic Ularn cavern generation restored; stairs always join the walkable maze
    • Cave 1 town exit stays connected; doors only at real junctions, facing the passage
    • Your adventurer name is remembered for the next expedition on this device
    • Classic floor loot amounts unchanged
  27. v1.3.17

    Maze floors, portals, and rare treasure rooms

    • Dungeon floors are maze-like again — rooms and corridors, doors that actually connect spaces
    • Classic loot amounts restored; item types still scale with depth
    • Only one town portal at a time; returning from town consumes it
    • Rare treasure rooms (0.10%) with real walls, more gold than items, and depth+3 monsters
  28. v1.3.16

    Loot goblin, shop clarity, and more maps

    • DnD shop shows proper scroll names and catalog prices; Town Portal stays 2500g on the last page
    • Buying or finding discovers items; undiscovered dungeon scrolls still share one graphic
    • Rare Loot Goblin flees, drops any item equally, and vanishes after 200 turns
    • Slight boosts to special weapon finds; 100 more unique maps plus 1% treasure maps
    • Larger unique weapon swing models and thin spell-aim grid lines
  29. v1.3.15

    What’s New on the site

    • New What’s New page with curated version notes for players
    • Changelog linked from the site nav, home, About, play welcome links, and footers
    • Clearer path to the GitHub repository and Releases
  30. v1.3.14

    Weapons, portals, and lighter meshes

    • Distinct trap meshes for pits, dart traps, and express elevators
    • Wielded weapons match floor loot art while swinging, with per-weapon attack sounds
    • Teleport to Town scroll at the DnD store; reading it opens linked dungeon and town portals
    • Undiscovered scrolls use shared unknown art until identified
    • Cheaper orb, ring, and cone meshes with tighter Balanced GPU budgets
  31. v1.3.13

    Traps, relics, and Field Guide tips

    • Trap art for pits, darts, and elevators
    • Special-item ground hovers with classic-tone titles for major relics
    • Short known-hazard tips; Field Guide sections for Hazards and Relics
    • Clearer journal lines when discovering or triggering hazards
  32. v1.3.12

    Site polish

    • Home gallery shows a clear town vs dungeon pair
    • Navbar keeps About, Play free, and Download for Windows
    • Footer adds a GitHub link to this repository
    • Favicon, apple-touch, and PWA icons regenerated from the brand sigil
  33. v1.3.11

    Balance and clarity

    • Sonic spear statue crumble chance raised; centipede and ant drain is a 20% roll
    • Undiscovered potions share unknown bottle art until learned
    • Minimap stairs: red down, green up; town buildings keep a clear empty ring
    • Self-cast spell buffs refresh instead of stacking
  34. v1.3.10

    Readable Balanced caves

    • Balanced quality raises ambient light so floors stay readable without torch point lights
    • Bloom is never allocated in Balanced; Cinematic builds a lighter pass only when chosen
    • Marketing home preview always runs Balanced
  35. v1.3.9

    Interactive hero and About history

    • Restored the interactive Three.js town preview on the marketing home
    • Creator history lives in About; legacy /credits URLs redirect there
    • Gallery screenshots refreshed
