Recent versions

From the current line.

Maintained alongside CHANGELOG.md in the repository. Older tags remain on GitHub Releases.

  1. v1.3.24

    Quiet rats on the walls

    • Small ambient rats scurry along dungeon wall tops — you cannot fight, loot, or click them
    • Only a handful appear at a time; town stays still
    • Labyrinth doors, textured floors, quieter saves, journal cap, XP panel, and classic loot unchanged
  2. v1.3.23

    Stable long expeditions

    • Long runs no longer slow the browser from an ever-growing journal — history stays deep and scrollable with a soft cap
    • Known-floor walks skip redundant map hashing; log snapshots reuse between paints
    • Textured caves, quiet saves, labyrinth doors, XP panel, and north camera unchanged
  3. v1.3.22

    Textured floors, quieter saves

    • Cave floors show classic stone texture again under flat lighting (no more solid white ground)
    • Walking no longer writes the expedition to disk every step — Save, exit, or leaving the tab persists
    • Labyrinth doors, XP panel, north camera, and classic loot density unchanged
  4. v1.3.21

    Door throats and labyrinth floors

    • Corridor doors seal flush with connecting stone — no diagonal keypad squeeze-arounds
    • Closed doors block corner-cuts; open a door to pass
    • Dungeon floors stay classic Ularn caverns but read more like a labyrinth (less blank stone, less wide-open halls)
    • Stairs, town exit, flat caves, XP panel, and classic loot density unchanged
  5. v1.3.20

    Smoother browser dungeon walking

    • Balanced graphics skips the shadow pass and tone-mapping cost that still hitch Chrome dungeon walks
    • Flat caves keep even lighting; stone floor maps restored in 1.3.22 after a temporary unmapped pass
    • Known floors reuse geometry and skip HUD/minimap thrash when only the adventurer moves
    • Dungeon walking is held to a town-relative frame budget in automated checks
  6. v1.3.19

    Experience panel, flat caves, north camera, door facing

    • Experience panel beside Spells and Stats with classic Ularn titles and XP progress
    • Even dungeon lighting — no torch hotspots, and zooming out no longer dims the cave
    • Camera starts facing north; your zoom and elevation preferences are remembered
    • Corridor doors face the hall correctly again
  7. v1.3.18

    Classic caverns, reachable stairs, remembered name

    • Classic Ularn cavern generation restored; stairs always join the walkable maze
    • Cave 1 town exit stays connected; doors only at real junctions, facing the passage
    • Your adventurer name is remembered for the next expedition on this device
    • Classic floor loot amounts unchanged
  8. v1.3.17

    Maze floors, portals, and rare treasure rooms

    • Dungeon floors are maze-like again — rooms and corridors, doors that actually connect spaces
    • Classic loot amounts restored; item types still scale with depth
    • Only one town portal at a time; returning from town consumes it
    • Rare treasure rooms (0.10%) with real walls, more gold than items, and depth+3 monsters
  9. v1.3.16

    Loot goblin, shop clarity, and more maps

    • DnD shop shows proper scroll names and catalog prices; Town Portal stays 2500g on the last page
    • Buying or finding discovers items; undiscovered dungeon scrolls still share one graphic
    • Rare Loot Goblin flees, drops any item equally, and vanishes after 200 turns
    • Slight boosts to special weapon finds; 100 more unique maps plus 1% treasure maps
    • Larger unique weapon swing models and thin spell-aim grid lines
  10. v1.3.15

    What’s New on the site

    • New What’s New page with curated version notes for players
    • Changelog linked from the site nav, home, About, play welcome links, and footers
    • Clearer path to the GitHub repository and Releases
  11. v1.3.14

    Weapons, portals, and lighter meshes

    • Distinct trap meshes for pits, dart traps, and express elevators
    • Wielded weapons match floor loot art while swinging, with per-weapon attack sounds
    • Teleport to Town scroll at the DnD store; reading it opens linked dungeon and town portals
    • Undiscovered scrolls use shared unknown art until identified
    • Cheaper orb, ring, and cone meshes with tighter Balanced GPU budgets
  12. v1.3.13

    Traps, relics, and Field Guide tips

    • Trap art for pits, darts, and elevators
    • Special-item ground hovers with classic-tone titles for major relics
    • Short known-hazard tips; Field Guide sections for Hazards and Relics
    • Clearer journal lines when discovering or triggering hazards
  13. v1.3.12

    Site polish

    • Home gallery shows a clear town vs dungeon pair
    • Navbar keeps About, Play free, and Download for Windows
    • Footer adds a GitHub link to this repository
    • Favicon, apple-touch, and PWA icons regenerated from the brand sigil
  14. v1.3.11

    Balance and clarity

    • Sonic spear statue crumble chance raised; centipede and ant drain is a 20% roll
    • Undiscovered potions share unknown bottle art until learned
    • Minimap stairs: red down, green up; town buildings keep a clear empty ring
    • Self-cast spell buffs refresh instead of stacking
  15. v1.3.10

    Readable Balanced caves

    • Balanced quality raises ambient light so floors stay readable without torch point lights
    • Bloom is never allocated in Balanced; Cinematic builds a lighter pass only when chosen
    • Marketing home preview always runs Balanced
  16. v1.3.9

    Interactive hero and About history

    • Restored the interactive Three.js town preview on the marketing home
    • Creator history lives in About; legacy /credits URLs redirect there
    • Gallery screenshots refreshed
  17. v1.3.8

    Marketing redesign

    • Multi-page site: home, play, About field guide, and creator history
    • New cave/gem brand sigil for favicon and PWA icons
    • SEO metadata, sitemap, and structured data for public pages
