Recent versions

From the current line.

Maintained alongside CHANGELOG.md in the repository. Older tags remain on GitHub Releases.

  1. v1.3.64

    A second look at the full cast

    • All eight classes have clearer silhouettes and details; the Rogue's hood fits its head.
    • All 66 enemies have distinct models, including the loot goblin. Serpents, insects, spirits, dragons and demon ranks read apart.
    • Wing membranes light correctly on both sides. Equipment, combat rules and visibility are unchanged.
    • Walking has less bounce and more natural knee and ankle movement. Quieter footsteps follow each landing, with softer exploration sounds.
    • Enemies ease into walking and settle naturally when they stop.
    • Sharper surface detail, deeper-looking walls and softer ground contact improve every area. Auto sharpens when there is room and lowers resolution when movement slows.
    • A simpler landing page and class portraits bring you into the world. Saved expeditions have a clear Continue action, and the landing preview allows phone scrolling.
  2. v1.3.63

    Graphics, controls and expedition records

    • Animated creature models and detailed swords join the current game, with Auto graphics and regional music.
    • Expand the compact map to plan a route; hold a direction to keep moving and release it to stop.
    • Background save compression keeps stable progress. Completed solo scores sync globally when online.
    • Online rooms keep verified scores, chat, spectators and host migration.
  3. v1.3.62

    A lemming is easy to hit

    • A level-1 adventurer with the starting dagger usually kills a lemming in one swing
    • That hit still finishes it. Other creatures are unchanged
  4. v1.3.61

    Quicker steps

    • You stop at a wall. The stride stays on open floor and does not draw you inside the rock
    • A step is quicker than before. A turn is still one move
    • The dagger is a thin edge. A dwarf still carries a spear, Rambo a lance, and Wizard, Elf, Ogre, and Klingon stay unarmed
  5. v1.3.60

    Expedition audio

    • Town and the caves play quiet original music after you turn sound on. The volcano uses the cave music, lower and dryer
    • A step, a sword swing, a hit, a door, stairs, and a spell each sound once with the action, then stop
    • Sound stays off the title screen. The expedition control still silences music and effects together
  6. v1.3.59

    A real swing and a real step

    • A weapon stroke winds up, cuts, and follows through. The dagger in hand is a short blade. A spear or lance stays long
    • Bare hands stay bare. Wizard, Elf, Ogre, and Klingon do not swing a sword they are not holding
    • Walking is a step, with a lift and a plant, instead of a slide. A turn is still one move
  7. v1.3.58

    Different wall rats

    • Fewer rats sit on dungeon walls. A corridor shows one or two, not a row of copies
    • Each rat has its own size, color, facing, and pose, so two in one scene are not the same drawing
    • They stay scenery. You still cannot fight, loot, or click them
  8. v1.3.57

    Fewer lemmings

    • A cave keeps one lemming, so they no longer stand in a ring on the tiles around you
    • That lemming is still the classic weak one: no hit points, no armor, and no damage
  9. v1.3.56

    Fantasy diorama

    • Stone and grass keep their pictures, with worn edges, moss, and dirt so floors repeat less
    • Town landmarks read apart: shop signs, the college banner, bank columns, the home garden, the cave mouth, and the volcano
    • The surface shaft is a laddered well, distinct from stairs and the town gate
    • The eight callings have their own silhouette, and the weapon in hand is the one you wield
    • Lemming, gnome, jackal, floating eye, and red dragon are small sculpted creatures. The other creatures keep their pictures
    • Fire, ice, lightning, and protective magic move differently, and Balanced stays the quiet preset
  10. v1.3.55

    Play screen links

    • About and What’s New sit at the top right, beside the field guide
    • History is gone from the play screen. Verify Download, the GitHub mark, and Github sit on one line under the Windows download
    • Sound stays off the title screen and returns once an expedition starts
  11. v1.3.54

    Rats in profile

    • The small rats on dungeon walls are a side-view pixel rat: brown body, pink ears, a dark eye, pink feet, and a long pink tail
    • They stay small, flat, and unlit, and you still cannot fight, loot, or click them
    • Town stays still
  12. v1.3.53

    Clearer steps

    • Sprites and you sit on whole pixels, so they stay sharp instead of soft or blocky
    • The stone picture on the floor and walls is the same, and it stays sharp
    • The camera and you ease onto the next step. A turn is still one move
  13. v1.3.52

    Travel past distant creatures

    • A walk stops for a creature only when it is on a neighboring tile, including diagonally
    • A creature down the hall, around a corner, or only on screen no longer ends the walk
    • Lemmings are placed less often, so a floor is not packed with them
  14. v1.3.51

    Visible version notes

    • What’s New shows the version list again
    • The notes were already on the page; they stayed blank when the list grew taller than the window
  15. v1.3.50

    Play title line

    • The play screen drops “Time moves only when you do.” and the version line under the links
    • The cave-and-gem mark sits on the Ularn 3D line, and “The caves below.” is smaller underneath
  16. v1.3.49

    Rats you can recognize

    • The small rats on dungeon walls have a head, ears, a body, and a tail
    • They stay flat and unlit, and you still cannot fight, loot, or click them
    • Town stays still
  17. v1.3.48

    Solid dungeon floors

    • Close views of the cave floor no longer show dark cracks between the stones
    • The stone picture on the floor and walls is the same. Balanced caves stay evenly lit
  18. v1.3.47

  19. v1.3.46

    • Home, About, and What’s New use one header and one footer
  20. v1.3.45

    Play page mark

  21. v1.3.44

    Start screen fits the window

    • The play screen shows the class choices, the class note, Multiplayer, Continue, and Download for Windows together on a normal desktop window
    • A saved expedition no longer pushes the Windows download below the fold
  22. v1.3.43

    Clearer download links

    • Links under Download for Windows read About, What’s New, History, Verify Download, then a GitHub icon, each on one line
  23. v1.3.42

    Chat emoji

    • Choosing an emoji puts it in the message at the cursor. Enter still sends
  24. v1.3.41

    Public end-screen scoreboard

    • If that board has no rows, it says the board is empty. If the board cannot be loaded, it says so
  25. v1.3.40

    Clearer chrome

    • Multiplayer, the lobby, and the leaderboard close with a round X. The title stays
    • Links under Download for Windows read About, New, History, Verify, then a GitHub icon, each on one line
    • The spectating line sits under the header on a blurred dark chip. The number is people watching, not the players or the host
  26. v1.3.39

    Live multiplayer rooms

    • Hosting, joining, and watching open the room and keep the lobby, chat, and turns in sync
    • Ready and Start talk to the room. If the host is gone, the next player can take the room
    • A finished co-op run is sent in for a verified or rejected score, and the leaderboard shows verified rows
    • Chat history stays with the people still in the room
  27. v1.3.37

    Verified scores

    • A finished run is replayed on the server. The number on the public board is the score that replay produced
    • A run that is too long to replay is refused. Shorter runs still count
    • A changed log, a run used twice, someone else's run, a co-op score from anyone but the host, and a run that finished too fast are still refused
  28. v1.3.36

    Score submit checks

    • A co-op score submission is checked against the current host of that room
    • Knowing another player's run id does not throw that run away
    • Too many score submissions in an hour are refused, and the run stays as it was
  29. v1.3.35

    Unknown finds and the aura switch

    • Picking up a potion or a scroll no longer tells you what it is. Drinking, reading, and an identify scroll still do. The shop still names what you buy
    • The floor bubble is only there in a multiplayer game, and it starts on. AURA: ON / AURA: OFF hides it. Off means creatures do not take that bubble's turn
    • A solo expedition has no bubble and no switch. Creatures still take their usual turn
  30. v1.3.34

    Online co-op

    • Optional multiplayer: host or join a room, watch a game, and chat with the party
    • Solo play is the same expedition. Online menus say “Online unavailable” when the service cannot be reached
    • A verified leaderboard is ready for scores the server has replayed. Unverified runs stay off the board
  31. v1.3.33

    Cooperation aura

    • A flat bubble follows you on the dungeon floor. It is 20 tiles wide and 10 tiles tall, centered on you with the extra tile to the east and north
    • The bubble is the ellipse of that rectangle. A creature on your floor is inside when its tile sits in the ellipse. The corners of the rectangle are outside it
    • When you take a turn, only creatures inside your bubble take a turn. Creatures outside stay put, and a creature on another floor never acts
    • Each creature acts once for the person who just moved
    • The bubble is a flat unlit shape. Balanced play stays evenly lit: no fog, no bloom, no shadow map, and no extra light
    • Town is unchanged
  32. v1.3.32

    Clearer caves

    • Hall floors and the rock around them use different tints on the same stone. The floor reads lighter than the wall
    • Deeper caves and the volcano use a warmer tint on that stone
    • Stairs, doors, and the town exit carry a small unlit mark. Gold and items sit a little brighter on the pictures they already use
    • A flat dark disc sits under you and under creatures, and you have a thin outline. It is not a cast shadow
    • The wall that drops in front of the camera keeps a shorter lip
    • Balanced play stays evenly lit: no fog when you zoom out, no bloom, and no shadow maps
  33. v1.3.31

    Monster hover card

    • Hovering a creature you can already see opens a small card with its sprite, its name, and a short original note
    • Hidden creatures and mimics stay as they are: no card and no extra facts
    • Floor items keep the one-line ground label
    • Hit points stay off the card. The game does not reveal that number
  34. v1.3.30

    Reachable caves

    • Every cave and volcano floor can be walked from where you enter. A room or hallway with no way in is joined by a door, or it is not built
    • Original chart floors keep their rooms. A sealed room gets a door into the cavern you can already travel
    • Stairs, monsters, and items go down after that, and only on tiles you can reach
    • Rare-item chances, combat, town, and teleport landings are unchanged
  35. v1.3.29

    Rare find rates

    • Brass lamp is rarer (5%). Slayer is 5 points rarer at every depth it can appear, and still starts on dungeon 10
    • Staff of power is 2 points rarer at every depth, and still starts on dungeon 8
    • Sword of slashing is 7.5%. Elven chain and the orb of enlightenment are 8.333%. Those are the closest steps on the 1–120 roll
    • Other rare items, caves, doors, combat, spells, and shops are unchanged
  36. v1.3.28

    Teleport landings

    • Teleport stays on the map. A scroll, spell, trap, or elevator never drops you outside the level
    • In town, you land in town. The square is not a cave, and the rock around it is not a floor
    • You arrive on open floor with a step back into the level. Rock, a closed door, and a walled-off pocket are skipped
    • A bad roll picks another open tile, or you stay put. It does not leave you in the wall
    • Caves, doors, combat, and rare-item chances are unchanged
  37. v1.3.27

    Reachable floors

    • Generated caves are kept only when you can walk the whole floor. A roll that traps rooms behind solid rock is discarded and rolled again
    • Stairs, monsters, and items go down after that, on tiles you can reach
    • Original chart floors are not redrawn. Their rock stays, and the stairs sit on the cavern you can travel
    • No extra tunnels or corridor doors. A treasure room still has one door, and only when it opens onto the maze
    • Combat, textures, and save timing are unchanged
  38. v1.3.26

    • Ordinary generated floors do not grow corridor doors. A treasure room has a single door in its wall
    • Rare artifacts use their original odds again. The brass lamp is unchanged
    • Combat, textures, and save timing are unchanged
