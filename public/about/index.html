About Ularn 3D

A classic turn-based roguelike in an overhead 3D world. Prepare in town, explore the caves, and make every move count.

Play in your browser ↗ Windows edition ↓

01 / Begin in town

Take your first steps.

Choose a name, class, and difficulty on the Ularn play page. The Adventurer is a balanced place to start. Town gives you room to find your bearings: stores, a bank, and a college offer ways to prepare before entering the caves.

Stand on a building or entrance and use its contextual action. Beneath town lie fifteen dungeon floors; the volcano adds five more. Your map reveals explored territory and uses the original game symbols. Monsters move and fight as you spend turns. You can stop to think without letting them take another turn.

Move into a monster to attack with your equipped weapon. Learn spells, manage your inventory, and watch the effects shown on the right side of the screen. Stronger enemies make preparation as useful as a good weapon.

02 / Choose your calling

Eight ways to enter the caves.

Each class starts with its own attributes and equipment.

Adventurer

A balanced traveler, capable with steel and spells.

Wizard

A brilliant spellcaster with powerful magic and fragile defenses.

Rogue

Nimble and clever, with dexterity as a particular strength.

Elf

A versatile spellcaster with a light touch in combat.

Dwarf

Sturdy and strong, built to endure the depths.

Ogre

Exceptional strength, with little aptitude for magic.

Klingon

A formidable fighter: strong and fierce, with limited wisdom.

Rambo

Weak starting attributes, paired with an extraordinary Lance of Death.

04 / Spells and saving

Magic with a direction. Progress that stays local.

Known spells appear in the game’s spell reference. Casting spends spell power, and some spells ask for a direction before they take effect. Watch the casting animation and visible trail to follow a projectile through the dungeon. Active buffs and conditions appear beside the play area.

The game saves automatically between actions. Use Save for a manual checkpoint, or Save & return to title from the menu. Return to the same browser and site, then choose Continue expedition. Browser saves live in that browser’s site storage. Desktop saves stay in your Windows profile and are separate from browser saves. Death ends the current run. There is no account or cloud sync.
