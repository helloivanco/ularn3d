01 / BEGIN IN TOWN

Take your first steps.

Choose a name, class and difficulty on the Ularn play page. The Adventurer is a balanced place to start. Town gives you room to find your bearings: stores, a bank and a college offer ways to prepare before entering the caves.

Stand on a building or entrance and use its contextual action. Beneath town lie fifteen dungeon floors; the volcano adds five more. Your map reveals explored territory and uses the original game symbols. Monsters move and fight as you spend turns. You can stop to think without letting them take another turn.

Move into a monster to attack with your equipped weapon. Learn spells, manage your inventory and watch the effects shown on the right side of the screen. Stronger enemies make preparation as useful as a good weapon.

04 / READY YOUR SPELLS

Magic with a direction.

Known spells appear in the game's spell reference. Casting spends spell power, and some spells ask you to choose a direction before they take effect. Watch the casting animation and visible trail to follow a projectile through the dungeon.

Active buffs and conditions appear beside the play area. Keep an eye on them before committing to a fight. An empty fountain stays visibly dry, and raised or descending stairs help you read a room at a glance.

05 / KEEP YOUR PLACE

Your adventure stays local.

The game saves automatically between actions. Use Save for a manual checkpoint, or Save & return to title from the menu. Return to the same browser and site, then choose Continue expedition.

Browser saves live in that browser's site storage. Clearing it removes those saves; another device or browser has its own progress. Death ends the current run. There is no account or cloud save synchronization.

A calmer view of a dangerous place.
